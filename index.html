Production Workflow

How We Work

From initial measurements to final electrical testing, our workflow ensures exact fit, durable materials, and on-time completion.

STEP 01

Enquiry & Consultation

Share your dimensions, photos, or architectural drawings via our quote form, phone, or WhatsApp.

STEP 02

Site Survey & Measurements

For Twin Cities projects, our technicians inspect the physical site, check structural wall framing, and verify electrical supply.

STEP 03

Written Quotation

We provide an itemized proposal specifying material thickness, steel gauge, LED module brand, controller model, and delivery schedule.

STEP 04

Artwork & Proof Approval

Our design team prepares a scale visual proof showing your branding, colors, and illumination simulation before cutting.

Workshop Fabrication

Laser cutting, CNC routing, channel bending, welding, and 48-hour electronic testing at our Committee Chowk workshop.

STEP 06

On-Site Installation

Structural mounting, safety wiring, controller setup, and handover across Rawalpindi and Islamabad.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding fabrication rates, SMD screen specifications, site visits, and delivery logistics.

Custom 3D sign boards and letters at Sign Out range from PKR 1,800 to PKR 4,000 per sq. ft. The rate varies based on face acrylic thickness (3mm–10mm cast acrylic), return material (acrylic, stainless steel hairline or mirror finish, or titanium gold), and whether lighting is frontlit, backlit (halo-lit), or non-illuminated.
SMD video walls are assembled from fixed module sizes (standard 320 × 160 mm). We calculate quotations based on the buildable cabinet grid that matches your desired aspect ratio, your required pixel pitch (P2/P2.5/P3/P4 for indoor, P4/P5/P8/P10 for outdoor daylight view), cabinet waterproofing, and Huidu Wi-Fi/Cloud controller hardware.
Yes. For clients in Rawalpindi and Islamabad, our technicians perform physical site surveys, laser measurements, heavy iron sub-framing, power supply cabling, and on-site mounting.
All out-of-city signage and SMD shipments are packed in heavy-gauge protective wooden crates with foam corner buffers to prevent transit impact. Cargo is dispatched via reputable freight carriers, with the exact cargo bill confirmed upon order booking.
We accommodate institutional batches as small as 20 printed RFID smart card sets for small offices or clinics, up to thousands for schools and universities. Roll-up standees can be ordered individually or in bulk exhibition sets.
Standard roll-up standees and card batches are completed within 24–48 hours. Custom 3D sign boards typically require 3 to 6 working days depending on scale, and SMD video screens require 4 to 8 working days including 48-hour burn-in testing. Commercial orders require an advance confirmation deposit with the balance due upon installation or dispatch.
